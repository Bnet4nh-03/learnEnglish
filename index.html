Loại Công Thức Cách Dùng Ví Dụ
Zero Conditional If + S + V(s/es), S + V(s/es) Sự thật hiển nhiên, quy luật If you heat ice, it melts.
Type 1 If + S + V(s/es), S + will + V Điều kiện có thể xảy ra trong tương lai If it rains, we will stay home.
Type 2 If + S + V2/ed, S + would + V Điều kiện không có thật ở hiện tại/tương lai If I were rich, I would buy a villa.
Type 3 If + S + had + V3, S + would have + V3 Điều kiện không có thật trong quá khứ If she had studied harder, she would have passed.
Mixed Conditional If + S + had + V3, S + would + V Quá khứ ảnh hưởng hiện tại If I had slept earlier, I would feel better now.
Unless Unless + S + V, S + will + V “Nếu không” Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.
Từ Cách Dùng Ý Nghĩa Ví Dụ
although / though Nối 2 ý trái ngược mặc dù Although it was raining, we went outside.
even though Nhấn mạnh hơn although mặc dù Even though he was tired, he kept working.
in spite of + noun / V-ing mặc dù In spite of the rain, they played football.
despite + noun / V-ing mặc dù Despite being sick, she went to school.
because Chỉ nguyên nhân bởi vì I stayed home because I was tired.
because of + noun / V-ing bởi vì The match was canceled because of the storm.
so Chỉ kết quả vì vậy He studied hard, so he passed the exam.
therefore Trang trọng hơn “so” do đó She was late; therefore, she missed the bus.
however Chỉ sự đối lập tuy nhiên He is rich; however, he is unhappy.
moreover / furthermore Thêm ý hơn nữa The job is well-paid. Moreover, it is flexible.
while Hai hành động đồng thời / đối lập trong khi She cooked while I cleaned.
whereas So sánh đối lập trong khi He likes tea, whereas I prefer coffee.
unless = if not nếu không Unless you hurry, you will be late.
instead of Thay vì thay vì She drank tea instead of coffee.
Từ Dùng Cho Chức Năng Ví Dụ
who Người Chủ ngữ The girl who is singing is my sister.
whom Người Tân ngữ The man whom I met was friendly.
whose Người / vật Sở hữu The boy whose bike was stolen cried.
which Vật / sự việc Chủ ngữ / tân ngữ The book which I bought is interesting.
that Người / vật Chủ ngữ / tân ngữ The movie that we watched was great.
where Địa điểm Nơi chốn This is the house where I was born.
when Thời gian Thời gian I remember the day when we met.
why Lý do Nguyên nhân I know the reason why she left.
Từ Cách Dùng Ví Dụ
that Thay thế người/vật đã xác định The car that I bought is new.
which Dùng cho vật, thường trong mệnh đề phụ The phone, which is expensive, is mine.
what = the thing that I know what you mean.
